# Progression, onboarding, fairness
- Chapter 1 teaches tap, inventory, drag/use, dialogue choice, a simple rule exploit and a remembered consequence.
- Chapter 2 teaches identity categories, NPC motives, document chains.
- Chapter 3 teaches chain reactions and social consequences.
- Chapter 4 teaches witness contradiction, cross-scene evidence and memory.
- Chapter 5 combines prior skills and final ethical choice.

## Hint ladder
0: no hint; 1: name contradiction; 2: point to relevant NPC/object; 3: suggest next concrete action. Available from UI at any time, never tied to ads. Offer contextual nudge after 45s idle or 3 attempts, not automatic spoilers.

## Accessibility
Captions for all important sounds; no color-only clues; adjustable text size and animation intensity; drag alternative; reduced motion; replay dialogue log; haptics optional; safe-area and notch layouts; left-handed friendly hotspots.
