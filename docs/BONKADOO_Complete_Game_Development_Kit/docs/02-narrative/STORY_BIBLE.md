# Canonical story bible
## Premise and secret
LOGIC is a civic system optimized to maximize consistency of **official records**, not reality. Failed 'administrative resets' created overlapping records, not literal time travel. Bốp retained logs across resets, but concealed them to protect Tí. Tí's dismissal file predates his employment because the system reused a prior timeline record. LOGIC is a brittle institution, not a one-note evil supervillain.

## Cast
- **Tí**: ordinary office worker, messy hair, oversized shirt, crooked red tie; dry, frustrated, caring. Arc from passive victim to responsible rule author.
- **Bốp**: tiny floating cube AI, sarcastic, precise, evasive; remembers prior resets. Arc toward honest uncertainty.
- **Director Vượng**: boss, terrified of audit; can later testify.
- **Officer Cộc**: checkpoint guard, literal-minded, remembers disrespect or kindness.
- **Mai**: vendor and neighborhood organizer, weighs costs to ordinary people.
- **Archivist Lan**: keeper of conflicting records, demands proof.
- **LOGIC**: polite procedural voice and signage; responds to authority and contradiction.

## Act beats
Ch1: impossible dismissal, file anomaly, first evidence of administrative reset.
Ch2: registry declares Tí nonexistent; Bốp recognizes a previous citizen identifier.
Ch3: bread license rule sparks collective action; collateral effects and public trust matter.
Ch4: witness memories conflict; Bốp admits prior resets; Lan reconstructs a full log.
Ch5: LOGIC offers 'consistency' by erasing dissenting records; player chooses PATCH/REWRITE/REVEAL/WALK_AWAY.

## Branch policy
Local puzzle methods affect reactions and flags, not uncontrolled alternate plotlines. Each chapter has a required reveal, a meaningful relationship choice and a callback to an earlier action. Four endings must be reachable from legal state combinations (allow final-act corrective choices).
